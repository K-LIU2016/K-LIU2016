## Completed / 已完成

### [Done&Go 探戈](https://github.com/K-LIU2016/Done-Go)

**Complete and being refined · 已完成，优化中**

A workspace that keeps core time records on your own device, for learning, research, teaching and knowledge work in the age of AI. Turn a sentence into a manageable task, check the result, record the time actually spent and decide how to use any time confirmed as freed up. The product has been delivered, with further refinements under way.

本地优先的时间工作台，面向学习、研究、教学和 AI 时代的知识工作：把一句话变成可完成的事项，核验结果，记录真实用时，再把确认腾出的时间归还给你。项目已完成交付，当前持续优化功能细节。

### [Phonics Game 自然拼读乐园](https://github.com/K-LIU2016/phonics-game)

**Completed demo · 已完成小样演示**

Three complete phonics mini-games in a single HTML file, free to use and with nothing to install, for children aged 3–5: turn over and match letters, listen and choose from eight themed word banks, and fill in missing letters using picture clues. The device’s own voice reads each word aloud, so an ordinary phone or an old laptop is all you need.

三个已完成的自然拼读小游戏装在一个 HTML 文件里，面向 3–5 岁、免安装、免费：字母记忆配对、八大主题词库听音辨词、看图补字母拼单词。每个单词都用设备自带语音朗读，一部普通手机或旧电脑就能玩。

Learning should not need a top-of-the-range phone. Open it in a browser and play offline once it has loaded. Feedback is immediate and spoken: a correct answer earns a star saved in the browser; a wrong answer simply means another go. There is no account, no advertising and no data collection.

学习不该需要一台旗舰手机：任意浏览器打开即玩，加载后可离线；反馈即时、听得见。答对会获得保存在浏览器中的星星，答错可以再试；无账号、无广告、不收集数据。

[Product details & screenshots / 产品详情与截图](https://github.com/K-LIU2016/phonics-game#readme)

### [Guiyi-Universe 归一](https://github.com/K-LIU2016/Guiyi-Universe)

**Completed demo · 已完成小样演示**

One question, one world: a 3D knowledge universe you can wander through. Ask a question and six floating islands rise from the sea: 来路 (the paths others have taken), 此地 (your circumstances), 岔路 (different viewpoints), 遇见 (real people), 未至 (blind spots) and 成形 (your own answer). Each island has its own activity, and Bloomy, a little robot with a green sprout on its head, keeps you company throughout.

一问一世界：一个可以走进去的 3D 知识宇宙。交给它一个问题，六座浮岛从海面升起——来路（别人怎么走过）、此地（你面对的条件）、岔路（分歧的走法）、遇见（真实的人）、未至（盲点）、成形（你自己的答案）。每座岛都有专属交互，头顶小芽的机器人 Bloomy 全程陪你探索。

The five-stage journey — ask, explore, compare, meet and form your own understanding — rewards noticing different perspectives rather than collecting points. The full journey works offline with built-in demo packs; progress is saved automatically in the browser.

五步旅程「提问 → 探索 → 对照 → 遇见 → 成形」奖励看见不同，而不是刷分。全程用内置演示问题包即可离线跑通，进度自动存档。

## Building / 在做

### [LearnLattice Infra](https://github.com/K-LIU2016/learnlattice-infra)

Evidence-aware education product intelligence with an interactive classroom runtime. **Learning Infrastructure** and the **LearnLattice Adapter** are self-built and maintained by Kira Liu. Learning Infrastructure is the design and evidence plane: describe the learner, setting, goals and constraints, then follow the reasoning from learning science through teaching interventions and product patterns to measurement and a reviewable blueprint. LearnLattice Adapter compiles that design, preserves provenance and human review, and connects it to **OpenMAIC**, the external interactive classroom runtime for stages, scenes, AI teacher and peer roles, learner interaction and export. OpenMAIC attribution: [THU-MAIC/OpenMAIC](https://github.com/THU-MAIC/OpenMAIC), with the associated paper in the [Journal of Computer Science and Technology](https://jcst.ict.ac.cn/en/article/doi/10.1007/s11390-025-6000-0). The first case is a ten-character introductory conversation course for children learning Mandarin as a second language.

LearnLattice Infra：以证据为基础的教育产品智能基础设施，并连接互动课堂运行层。**Learning Infrastructure** 与 **LearnLattice Adapter** 两层均由 Kira Liu 自建并维护。Learning Infrastructure 是设计与证据层：描述学习者、情境、目标和限制，沿着“学习科学 → 教学干预 → 产品模式 → 测量 → 可审查蓝图”追踪设计依据；LearnLattice Adapter 负责编译设计、保留来源链与人工审核，并接入 **OpenMAIC** 这一外部互动课堂运行层，负责 Stage、Scene、AI 教师与同伴角色、学习者互动和导出。OpenMAIC 出处：[THU-MAIC/OpenMAIC](https://github.com/THU-MAIC/OpenMAIC)，相关论文见 [Journal of Computer Science and Technology](https://jcst.ict.ac.cn/en/article/doi/10.1007/s11390-025-6000-0)。首个案例为汉语二语儿童学习者的十个汉字初次交流课程。

### [Oracle Land 神谕之地](https://github.com/K-LIU2016/Oracle-Land)

**Building · 在做**

A 3D flip-book of 50 Chinese pictographs for learners of Chinese as a second language. Each character writes itself stroke by stroke in brush script, steps back three thousand years into its oracle-bone or bronze form, then rises off the page as the thing it pictures — the sun, a river, a horse, a cart — with bilingual annotations and the story of how the picture became the character.

五十个象形字的立体翻页练习册，面向汉语二语学习者：每个字先按笔顺一笔一笔写成楷书，退回三千年前的甲骨文或金文，再从纸上立起，成为它所画的实物——太阳、河流、马、车——附中英标注与“图画如何变成字”的字源故事。

Every character returns inside a classical poem, followed by six small exercises and a final compound lab where two pictographs make a new character. Everything is generated in code, as one HTML file with no image or model assets, so it runs offline on ordinary classroom devices.

每个字还会在一首古诗里再次出现，随后配有六项小练习，最后是组字实验室：两个象形字组成一个新字。全部程序化生成：单 HTML 文件、零图片模型素材，普通教室设备可离线运行。
