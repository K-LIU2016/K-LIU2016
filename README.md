**Advancing educational equity through practice; exploring AI interpretability, brain science and neuroscience.**

Ten years of teaching in Thailand, the UK, Russia and China have left me with a few questions I could happily spend a lifetime exploring. What makes a child want to get to the bottom of something? What helps them have another go when it feels difficult? And what would help more children reach that small but rather wonderful ‘Hang on — I think I’ve got it!’ moment?

我在泰国、英国、俄罗斯和中国的课堂里，走过了十年。不同的语言、文化和孩子，让我不断回到几个愿意用很长时间追问的问题：是什么让一个孩子想要弄明白？遇到困难时，什么能让他们愿意再试一次？我们又怎样让更多孩子拥有那个小小的、却很有力量的瞬间——「等一下，我好像懂了！」

**These days, those questions find their way into my code, too.** Sometimes that means a phonics game you can play on an old laptop. Sometimes it means a little universe you can wander round, or a tool intended to give you a bit more time in your day. With LearnLattice Infra, I’m exploring how learning science can inform lesson plans, activities and learning we can actually observe. This GitHub is the workshop where those ideas gradually take shape.

**现在，我也把这些问题写进代码里。** 有时，它是一款旧电脑也能玩的自然拼读游戏；有时，是一个可以走进去、四处探索的知识宇宙；有时，是一个希望给人多留一点时间的工具。在 LearnLattice Infra 中，我尝试让学习科学一路走进教案、活动和可以观察的学习过程。这里的 GitHub，就是这些尝试慢慢生长的工作间。

I like playful ideas and questions that refuse to leave me alone. Could getting something wrong open up an interesting new route? Could teachers spend less time cobbling materials together and more time noticing the children in front of them? Could a learner leave with a better question than the one they arrived with? I enjoy making things that invite people to click, have a go and think a little — ideally followed by, ‘Ooh, can I do that again?’

我喜欢好玩的点子，也喜欢那些不肯轻易放过我的问题。答错一次，能不能开启一条有意思的新路？老师能不能少花一点时间拼凑材料，多一点时间看见眼前的孩子？学习者离开时，能不能带走一个比来时更好的问题？我喜欢做让人愿意点一点、试一试、想一想的东西，最好还能听到一句：「咦！我可以再玩一次吗？」

**I take curiosity seriously, and evidence just as seriously.** A lovely demo makes me want to have a proper look: what did the learner actually learn? Which part helped? And which bits am I still taking on trust? My research in digital teaching and learning, alongside the questions that come up in classrooms and makerspaces, keeps me in that habit. I want to keep the enthusiasm to build an idea and the honesty to rethink it when the evidence suggests I should.

**我认真对待好奇心，也认真对待证据。** 一个漂亮的演示会让我想继续往下问：学习者究竟学会了什么？哪一部分帮到了他们？哪些判断还只是我的假设？数字教学与学习的研究，以及课堂和创客空间里的问题，都在提醒我保持这种习惯。我希望自己始终有热情把一个想法做出来，也有诚实面对证据、重新修改它的勇气。

Education runs through everything I make. I want teachers to have more time and energy to teach, children to feel comfortable asking questions they haven’t quite worked out yet, and ordinary devices in ordinary classrooms to offer learning worth spending time on. I’m still learning how to get there. There will be prototypes, surprises and ideas that need a rethink. I’m happy to keep at it: ask a clearer question, make the next version a little better, and go again.

教育，是串起这些事情的那根线。我希望老师有更多时间和精力投入教学，孩子可以安心地问出还没想清楚的问题，普通的设备、普通的教室，也能承载值得投入的学习。我还在学习怎样一步步接近这些愿望。这一路会有小样、有惊喜，也会有需要推倒重想的地方。我愿意持续做下去，把问题问得更清楚，把下一个版本做得更好一点。

- [Phonics Game 自然拼读乐园](https://github.com/K-LIU2016/phonics-game) — **Completed Demo · 已完成小样演示**

  Three complete phonics mini-games in a single HTML file, free to use and with nothing to install, for children aged 3–5: turn over and match letters, listen and choose from 8 themed word banks, and fill in missing letters using picture clues. The device’s own voice reads each word aloud, so an ordinary phone or an old laptop is all you need. `HTML · Web Speech API · localStorage` <br> 三个已完成的自然拼读小游戏装在一个 HTML 文件里，面向 3–5 岁、免安装、免费：字母记忆配对、8 大主题词库听音辨词、看图补字母拼单词——每个单词都用设备自带语音朗读，一部普通手机或旧电脑就能玩。

  Learning shouldn’t need a top-of-the-range phone. Open it in a browser and play offline once it has loaded. Feedback is immediate and spoken: a correct answer earns a star saved in the browser; a wrong answer simply means another go, with no penalty. The word banks include 26 illustrated alphabet words (A-Apple … Z-Zebra), around 24 CVC words, and themes covering animals, food, numbers, colours, nature and the body, plus a mixed challenge. No account, no adverts and no data collection.

  <br>学习不该需要一台旗舰手机：任意浏览器打开即玩，加载后可离线；反馈即时、听得见——答对朗读单词并奖励保存在浏览器的星星，答错可以再试、不惩罚；词库覆盖 26 个字母图片词（A-Apple … Z-Zebra）、约 24 个 CVC 词，以及动物、食物、数字、颜色、自然、身体主题词库与混合挑战；无账号、无广告、不收集数据。

- [Guiyi-Universe 归一](https://github.com/K-LIU2016/Guiyi-Universe) — **Completed Demo · 已完成小样演示**

  One question, one world: a 3D knowledge universe you can wander through. Ask a question and six floating islands rise from the sea: 来路 (the paths others have taken), 此地 (your circumstances), 岔路 (different viewpoints), 遇见 (real people), 未至 (blind spots) and 成形 (your own answer). Each has an activity of its own: a story trail, a console for exploring conditions, branching bridges, a campfire, a gate in the mist and a wall for connecting ideas. Bloomy, a little robot with a green sprout on its head, keeps you company throughout. `JavaScript · Three.js · Vite · Node.js` <br> 一问一世界：一个可以走进去的 3D 知识宇宙。交给它一个问题，六座浮岛从海面升起——来路（别人怎么走过）· 此地（你面对的条件）· 岔路（分歧的走法）· 遇见（真实的人）· 未至（盲点）· 成形（你自己的答案），六岛各有专属交互（故事小径、条件控制台、分岔桥、篝火围坐、迷雾光门、关联壁），头顶小芽的机器人 Bloomy 全程陪你走完。

  The five stages — 提问 → 探索 → 对照 → 遇见 → 成形 (ask, explore, compare, meet and form your own understanding) — reward noticing different perspectives rather than racking up points. Label the cards you collect ‘agree’ (认同), ‘not sure’ (存疑) or ‘surprised’ (惊讶); compare the assumptions behind them without declaring a winner; and meet people whose cards ask you questions in return. Finish with your own answer as it stands, ready to revise whenever you like. The full journey works offline with built-in demo packs. With the local backend running online, Zhihu search and a language model generate question packs with sources and a plan for each island. Progress is saved automatically in the browser.

  <br>五步旅程「提问 → 探索 → 对照 → 遇见 → 成形」奖励看见不同，而不是刷分：收下的卡片可贴态度贴纸（认同 / 存疑 / 惊讶），对照只指出前提差异、不裁决胜负，遇见的人会反过来追问你，结束时写下属于你此刻、可随时修改的答案。全程用内置演示问题包即可离线跑通；本机后端在线时，知乎检索 + 大模型生成带真实来源与六岛计划的问题包；进度自动存档。
